## Personal loans
Common application documents may include government identity and address proof, income or salary records, and recent bank statements. Requirements vary by lender and applicant; confirm the current checklist with the lender.

Eligibility can depend on age, income, employment, credit history, existing obligations, and the lender's current policy. Only the lender can confirm eligibility after reviewing an application.

Loan interest rates can depend on the lender, applicant profile, principal, tenure, credit history, and product terms. Check the current rate, fees, annual percentage cost, and repayment schedule with the lender before borrowing.

An equated monthly installment (EMI) is a scheduled repayment that usually includes principal and interest. The amount depends on the principal, interest rate, and tenure. Review the lender's amortization schedule and total repayment before accepting a loan.

Before early or partial loan repayment, check the loan agreement for permitted timing, notice requirements, charges, and how the payment affects principal and future installments. Terms vary by lender and product.

## Accounts and KYC
A savings account is generally intended for personal deposits and payments and may earn interest under the provider's terms. A current account is generally used for frequent business transactions. Features, fees, eligibility, and interest vary by institution.

KYC means Know Your Customer. Financial institutions use identity and customer information checks to meet applicable rules and reduce fraud. Required documents and update procedures vary; use the institution's official channels.

Account statements are commonly available through official online banking, mobile apps, branches, or customer support. Availability, statement periods, and any fees depend on the institution.

## Cards and credit
If a debit or credit card is lost or stolen, contact the issuer promptly through its official app, phone number, or branch to block it and ask about replacement. Never share a PIN, password, or one-time passcode.

For an unrecognized card transaction, promptly lock or block the card if available and report the transaction to the issuer through official channels. Keep the reference number and follow the issuer's dispute process and deadlines.

A credit score is a summary measure based on information in a credit report. Payment history, credit utilization, account age, and recent applications can affect scores, depending on the scoring model. Check your report with an authorized credit bureau and correct errors through its process.

The minimum due is the smallest amount required by the due date under the card terms; paying only that amount can leave a balance accruing interest. The total due is the full statement balance. Check the card agreement for rates, fees, and payment allocation.

## Deposits and payments
Deposit protection, where available, is subject to jurisdiction-specific limits, eligible institution and product rules, and account ownership rules. Confirm coverage and current limits with the official deposit-protection authority; this assistant does not determine coverage.

Before opening a fixed or term deposit, compare the effective interest rate, term, early withdrawal rules, penalties, renewal instructions, tax treatment, and deposit protection. Rates and terms change; confirm them with the institution.

For digital payments, use the official banking or payment app, verify the recipient and amount before approving, secure your device, and never share a PIN, password, or one-time passcode. A payment PIN is used to authorize payments; it is not needed to receive money.

If a digital payment is pending or failed, check the official app for status and reference details, avoid repeating a debit that may still settle, and contact the payment provider or bank through official channels if it is not resolved within their stated timeframe.

## Digital banking and security
Protect mobile banking with a device screen lock, updated software, the official app, and unique credentials. Enable alerts where offered, avoid public devices and untrusted links, and never disclose passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes, including to someone claiming to be support.

Phishing messages often create urgency, impersonate a bank, or direct customers to a link or number to disclose credentials. Do not open suspicious links or attachments. Contact the institution using the number on its official website, card, or app.

If you suspect account fraud, contact the bank immediately through official channels, secure or block affected credentials and cards, preserve transaction details, and follow the bank's reporting process. Use the relevant official government or payment-system reporting channel where applicable.

## Insurance
Before buying insurance, review the coverage, exclusions, waiting periods, limits, deductibles, premiums, renewal terms, cancellation rules, and claim process. Disclose requested information accurately and compare the policy wording, not only the headline price. Terms are insurer-specific.

Starting an insurance claim may require the policy number, claimant details, date and description of the event, and supporting documents such as bills or reports. Requirements and deadlines depend on the policy and insurer; notify the insurer promptly and use its official claim process.

## Investing
Investments with higher potential returns often involve greater risk, including loss of principal; returns are not guaranteed unless the specific product terms and applicable protections state otherwise. Time horizon, liquidity needs, fees, and ability to tolerate loss matter. This is general education, not personalized investment advice.

Diversification means spreading investments across different assets or exposures to reduce reliance on any single one. It cannot remove all investment risk or guarantee a profit. Consider costs, time horizon, and personal circumstances before investing.

## Customer support and privacy
For account-specific decisions, current rates or fees, disputes, suspected fraud, or product terms not included here, contact the institution's authorized customer support through official channels.

Account, transaction, card, and identity details are private. Never share full account or card numbers, passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes in chat. This demonstration assistant uses sample tool data and is not connected to a live customer account.
